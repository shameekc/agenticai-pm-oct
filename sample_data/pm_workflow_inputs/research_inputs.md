# Wren CX — Raw Research Inputs (for Workflow 1: Research Synthesis)
*Fictional. Collected from 6 brand-side support leads, Sep 2026.*

## Interview snippets
- **Support lead, fashion D2C (Jaipur):** "Half my team's morning is 'where is my order'. We copy-paste the tracking link 300 times a day."
- **CX head, electronics D2C (Bengaluru):** "I'm terrified of a bot promising a replacement. Last year an agent promised one by mistake and we had to honour it — ₹38,000."
- **Founder, beauty D2C (Mumbai):** "Customers write in Hinglish on WhatsApp. Every tool we tried treated those messages as low priority or 'unknown'."
- **Ops manager, home goods (Pune):** "Escalations get lost. The angry customer emails five times and each email lands with a different agent."
- **Support lead, kidswear (Delhi):** "If the bot handles 70% I'm happy, but I need to see *why* it did what it did when a customer complains."
- **CX head, appliances (Chennai):** "Safety complaints — sparks, burns — have to reach me in minutes, not in the next shift."

## Support ticket themes (last 30 days, one brand)
- Where is my order: 41% · Returns/exchange: 18% · Refund status: 14% · Product questions: 11% · Complaints: 9% · Billing/EMI: 5% · Spam/other: 2%
