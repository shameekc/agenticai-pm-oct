# Competitor Release Notes (for Workflow 3: Competitive Intelligence)
*Fictional composite of a global helpdesk vendor's Q3 2026 announcement.*

**"Introducing Autopilot Resolve — AI agents that close tickets, not just answer them."**

- Autopilot Resolve now takes actions in connected order systems: cancel orders, issue refunds up to a merchant-set limit, and schedule return pickups — no human in the loop for amounts under the limit.
- New "Policy Studio": merchants write refund/return policy in plain English; the agent enforces it.
- Supports 12 new languages including Hindi and Tamil (Hinglish "coming soon").
- Pricing moves from per-seat to per-resolution: $0.99 per ticket the AI fully resolves.
- Audit log: every AI action is recorded with the policy clause it relied on.
- Available on Enterprise plans first; mid-market in Q1 2027.
