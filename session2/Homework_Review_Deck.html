Tonight's MCP block, in one bug

MCP gives errors a shape. You decide the rest.

What the protocol gives you

  • A tool schema with required fields
  • A standard error envelope
  • The call fails loudly, not silently

What the PM must spec

  • Collect required fields before the call
  • Never show raw errors to customers
  • Fallback: “not done yet” + a human route
