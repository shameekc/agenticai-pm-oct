The Contract

By 31 October, your capstone team will present:

  1. A working agent for your capstone problem — not a screenshot. Something that runs, live.
  2. An AI-native PRD — I/O contract, quality criteria with numbers, failure modes with fallbacks.
  3. An eval harness — a golden dataset and an LLM-as-judge you can run against any version of your agent.
  4. Guardrails, a HITL design and a cost model — what it refuses, when a human steps in, what it costs per month.

We are not here to understand agentic AI in theory. We are here to ship a slice of it — and know exactly what it takes to ship the rest.

One Rule
"Build first.
Polish never."

Every exercise in this room produces something that runs. A broken agent that does half the job is worth more than a perfect spec that does none of it.

  • Imperfect and working beats perfect and theoretical — every time
  • If you're stuck for more than 5 minutes, call it out — the room will help
  • Online? Use the chat or unmute — don't silently struggle
  • Post blockers in the chat — someone in the cohort has usually solved it

You are PMs. You know how to ship under constraints. The constraint today is time — not capability. Let's go.
