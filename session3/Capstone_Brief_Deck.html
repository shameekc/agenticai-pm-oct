On the day

Three rules, and the questions you'll get

Rule 1

Demo live with a hard input

Injection, high stakes, missing information, or an edge case from your domain.

Rule 2

Show a guardrail catch something

On screen, not described.

Rule 3

Own every number

Every threshold and cost has a reason you can say out loud.

  • "Where does that threshold come from? What does a false negative cost you?"
  • "What breaks if you remove one of your agents?"
  • "What did your judge get wrong? How do you know it agrees with a human?"
  • "What happens to your monthly cost at 10× volume?"
